What we actually arrange

A better base for the ES Happy Tours package you booked.

The accommodation details support the important parts of the trip: early call times, ferry schedules, south Cebu routing, and clean drop-off plans after long tour days.

Before booking the room

Tell us the tour first, then we guide the stay area.

Cebu City is usually practical for city tours and pier days. South Cebu overnights can help with Moalboal or Oslob call times. Bohol stays depend on ferry timing and the countryside route. We keep the advice tied to your ES Happy Tours package and the way your group wants to travel.

01

Cebu City base

Good for arrivals, city tours, airport pickups, and Bohol pier transfers. It also keeps the first day flexible when your group is still settling in.

  • Pickup from hotel, airport, or agreed Cebu City point
  • Works well before city tour, Bohol, or south Cebu routes
  • Easy drop-off at hotel, SM Cebu, or pier area when needed
02

South Cebu overnight

A nearby stay can make early Moalboal or Oslob days easier, especially for groups who want less road time before the ocean activities.

  • Better pacing for sardines run, turtles, Kawasan, or Oslob
  • Early morning pickup guidance by area
  • Drop-off planning if another tour follows the next day
03

Bohol stay timing

Bohol is mostly about timing: ferry, countryside distance, and the return trip to Cebu. We help you avoid choosing a stay area that makes the tour day harder than it needs to be.

  • Cebu pier and Bohol arrival coordination
  • Countryside route planning
  • Return ferry and Cebu drop-off guidance
